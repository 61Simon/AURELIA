Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to eliminate swirl marks, micro-scratches, and oxidation. We refine the clear coat to reveal a flawless, mirror-like finish.

Ceramic Coating

Professional-grade SiO₂ coatings that form a permanent, hydrophobic layer. Enhances gloss, provides extreme chemical resistance, and makes maintenance effortless.

Paint Protection Film

Optically clear, self-healing urethane film applied to high-impact areas or the full body. Acts as an invisible shield against road debris and stone chips.

Who We Are

About Aurelia

A Studio Built on Obsession, Not Shortcuts.

AURELIA was founded on a simple belief: a car is more than transportation. It is design, engineering, and personal expression. Every vehicle that enters our studio is treated as a unique canvas — inspected under controlled lighting, corrected by hand, and protected with materials chosen for longevity, not convenience.

We work in a climate-controlled, dust-managed environment. Our specialists train continuously on new paint systems, film technologies, and ceramic chemistries. We do not rush. We do not use fillers to hide defects. We measure, polish, and verify — panel by panel.

From daily-driven performance cars to collector-grade exotics, our philosophy remains the same: understand the surface, respect the material, and deliver a finish that looks intentional from every angle.

FAQ

Answers Before You Book

For a daily-driven car, a full detail every 6–12 months is typical, with safe maintenance washes in between. Vehicles with ceramic coating often need less frequent deep cleaning, but annual inspections are still recommended.

Professional coatings range from 2 to 5 years depending on the product, preparation, and maintenance. We offer both entry-level and long-life options, and we explain the realistic durability for your usage before you choose.

When performed by a trained specialist with paint depth measurement and appropriate pads and polishes, correction is safe. The goal is to remove defects while preserving as much clear coat as possible.

Ceramic coating is a liquid SiO₂ product that cures to a thin, hydrophobic layer. It enhances gloss and makes washing easier but does not stop stone chips. PPF is a thick, clear urethane film that physically absorbs impacts.

A premium wash takes 2–3 hours. Paint correction and coating typically require 1–2 days. Full packages with PPF can take 2–3 days. We provide a realistic timeline after inspection.
